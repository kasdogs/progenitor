Using Progenitor

Playing

Click or touch the keys (lower on the key plays louder), or play two octaves from the computer keyboard: the Z row and A row start on the low F, and the Q row and number row continue an octave up, covering all 32 keys (labels are on the keys). ← / → are the <KB / KB> octave buttons. Space is PLAY.

Scale lock (above the keys) snaps every key to the nearest note of the chosen mode and key; Custom lets you click keys to pick the notes. It applies to the panel and computer keyboards, not MIDI input.

Knobs

Drag up/down (or sideways; on phones, up/down only, since sideways swipes scroll the panel). Hold Shift for very fine adjustment; on touch screens, tap the panel's SHIFT button to latch it. Double-click resets. The mouse wheel and arrow keys also work.

Patching

Drag from one jack to another. Or tap a jack, then tap its destination. Any jack can connect to any other, and every jack takes as many cables as you like: dragging always starts a new one. With a mouse, click (or right-click) a patched jack to open its disconnect menu: it lists every cable on that jack, so you pull exactly the one you mean (hovering a row highlights that cable). Alt-drag moves a plug; drop it in empty space to pull it. On touch screens a tap always selects a jack, and it stays selected while you scroll: tap another jack to connect, or the same jack again to cancel (tap the bar at the bottom to scroll back to it). To drag a cable, drag up or down from a jack, or touch and hold briefly and then drag in any direction; a quick sideways swipe scrolls instead. Press and hold a patched jack and let go for its disconnect menu, or hold and drag to move its newest plug. Dragging a cable to the edge of the screen scrolls the panel. On phones the buttons along the top jump between modules, and KEYS hides or shows the keyboard (more room for patching). Pinch the modules to zoom them (the page itself doesn't zoom); ⋯ → Reset module zoom fits them to the screen height again. Everything joined by cables is solved as one passive circuit, using the impedances in the Grandmother manual: audio outputs are 2 kΩ, control outputs 5 Ω, inputs 90–110 kΩ. Two audio outputs in the mult mix half and half, a control output joined to anything wins, fighting control outputs meet in the middle, and an input joined only to other inputs reads 0 V. As the manual advises, only audio outputs are meant to be merged. Patching into an input overrides the internal connection behind it, like the hardware's normalled routing. The four MULT jacks are wired together. Outputs have dark labels.

Arpeggiator and sequencer

Choose ARP or SEQ, press PLAY, then hold notes. HOLD latches. TAP three times sets tempo; hold TAP for a second to leave tap tempo.

To record: set REC, pick a slot with OCT/SEQ, and play notes. While in REC, PLAY adds a TIE, HOLD adds a REST, TAP sets an ACCENT. Holding one note while pressing the next records legato. Switch back to SEQ and press PLAY. Switching to REC while a sequence plays lets you overwrite steps live.

Accents appear at KB VEL OUT while sequencing; patch it to CUTOFF IN to hear them.

Hidden gestures

Hold HOLD and turn GLIDE right/left to switch legato glide on/off. Hold HOLD + press SYNC for global settings. Hold PLAY, HOLD and TAP together for a second to clear stuck notes. Tap the SPRING REVERB title to give the tank a knock.
